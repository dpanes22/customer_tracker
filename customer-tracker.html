Setup Guide
Configuration

1. Create a free JSONBin.io account

  1. Go to jsonbin.io and sign up for a free account.
  2. Verify your email and log in to the dashboard.

2. Create a Bin

  1. Click "Create a Bin" in the dashboard.
  2. Set the initial content to {} and click Create.
  3. Copy the Bin ID shown in the URL or bin details panel.

3. Get your Master Key

  1. Go to API Keys in your account settings.
  2. Copy your Master Key (starts with $2b$10$…).
  3. Paste both values in the Configuration tab and click Connect.

Free tier limits

10,000 reads / month  ·  10,000 writes / month  ·  1 GB storage — more than enough for this app.
